# Neighbors in the Loop brand

## Identity

The logo is built from two connected loops with a warm center point. It represents local discovery, recurring neighborhood connections, and the people at the center of community life.

## Assets

- `logo.svg`: primary full-color mark for the app shell and favicon
- `logo-mono.svg`: single-color mark for dark, light, or constrained surfaces
- `og-image.svg`: social preview artwork for shared links

## Palette

- Forest: `#344C2D`
- Sage: `#89A982`
- Soft cream: `#F6F8EE`
- Warm clay: `#D89062`
- Body green: `#16391F`

## Usage

Keep clear space around the mark equal to the width of the center dot. Use the full-color mark on light surfaces and the monochrome mark when the background or production system controls the color. Do not stretch, rotate, or place the mark on a busy image.
